How it works

A verb drops from the top of the screen. Decide which pattern follows it, then swipe or tap before it hits the bottom.

←

Swipe left for -ing

Verbs like enjoy take a gerund: enjoy swimming, avoid waiting.

→

Swipe right for to + verb

Verbs like want take an infinitive: want to swim, decide to stay.

⏱

Beat the clock

45 seconds and 3 lives. Combos add bonus points, and the words you miss come back more often.

Two ways to play

Start with the basics, then take on the verbs that change meaning.

Classic

Two choices: gerund or infinitive.

  • -ing I enjoy reading.
  • to + verb She hopes to travel.

Four ways

Harder verbs that take both forms.

  • ↑ same meaning love swimming / love to swim
  • ↓ different meaning stop smoking / stop to smoke

Learn while you play

Every round ends with a short review of your mistakes, with an example sentence for each verb.
